Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new company gets a 10-day free trial with no payment details needed. Choose a plan only if you want to continue after the trial.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

What about refunds?

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any charge. See our Refund Policy.

How do I pay, and what about tax?

Payments are handled by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com Market Limited. Prices are in US dollars, and Paddle adds any sales tax or VAT that applies in your country at checkout, before you pay.
