Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

A simple 14-day refund promise for Nexora AdPilot subscriptions, and how to ask for one.

01Our promise

We want you to be happy with Nexora AdPilot. If it is not right for you, you can ask for a refund of your payment within 14 days of the charge, and we will approve it. No complicated questions.

02Free trial first

Every new company gets a 10-day free trial with no payment details needed. We encourage you to use it to check AdPilot fits your business before you subscribe.

03How refunds work

  • Monthly and yearly plans: you can request a full refund within 14 days of any charge, including a renewal charge.
  • After 14 days: we do not refund the current billing period, but you can cancel at any time and keep using your plan until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Cancelling: cancelling stops future renewals. It does not by itself refund a payment already made.

04How to ask for a refund

Email us at [email protected] from the address on your account, with your company name and the date of the payment. You can also request it directly from Paddle using your receipt email, which has a link to Paddle’s buyer support.

Payments are processed by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com Market Limited, so refunds are issued by Paddle to your original payment method. It usually takes a few business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer. Refunded tax is returned together with the payment.

05If something went wrong

If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or you cannot use the Service because of a fault on our side, tell us and we will put it right, including a refund where that is the fair result, even if it is outside the 14 days.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the law where you live.
