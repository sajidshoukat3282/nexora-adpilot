Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

How Nexora Technologies collects, uses and protects personal information when you use Nexora AdPilot and this website.

01Who we are

This policy explains how Nexora Technologies, Lahore, Pakistan (“Nexora”, “we”) collects and uses personal information when you visit nexoratechnologies.pk or use Nexora AdPilot. For personal information in AdPilot accounts, Nexora is responsible for the account and billing details described below, and your company decides what customer and business data it puts into the workspace.

02Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email address, company name, role and sign-in information. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Workspace content: the data and files you or your team add to AdPilot, such as screens, campaigns, clients, proposals, invoices and photos.
  • Billing information: your plan, billing period, subscription status and payment identifiers sent to us by Paddle. Your card details are collected and processed by Paddle and are not stored by us.
  • Usage and technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser type, pages or features used, and error information, which we use to keep the Service secure and working.
  • Messages: what you send us by email, WhatsApp or the contact form.

03How we use it

  • to create and run your account and provide the Service;
  • to send emails you need, such as verification, sign-in links and password resets;
  • to process your subscription and keep billing records;
  • to give support and respond to your messages;
  • to protect the Service, prevent misuse and fix problems;
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the content of your workspace for advertising.

04Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run AdPilot. They only get the information they need to do their job:

  • Cloudflare for hosting, database and file storage;
  • Brevo for sending transactional emails;
  • Paddle.com Market Limited as our Merchant of Record, which handles payments, tax and invoices. Paddle acts as an independent controller of your payment information; see Paddle’s privacy policy;
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google;
  • map and weather data providers, which receive location queries needed for features such as screen weather.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users. If our business is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

05Where your information is processed

Our providers run global networks, so your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, including outside Pakistan. We choose providers that apply appropriate security and data protection measures.

06Cookies

AdPilot uses a sign-in session cookie and similar storage that are necessary for you to stay logged in and use the app. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will describe it here.

07How long we keep it

We keep account and workspace data while your account is active. If you close your account, we keep your data for a short period so you can export it and then delete it. We keep billing and tax records for as long as the law requires.

08Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, separation between companies’ data and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

09Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to stop certain uses of it. Account owners can export all workspace data from the Admin area at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first, and we may keep some information where the law requires.

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it.

10Children

AdPilot is for businesses and is not meant for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page with its date. If a change is significant we will notify account owners by email.
