Nexora AdPilot

Advertising management software for out-of-home (OOH) and digital signage (DOOH) companies

Nexora AdPilot is a subscription web application (SaaS). Advertising companies use it to manage their screens and inventory, campaigns, proposals, invoices, client reporting and field operations in one place. Start with a free trial, then choose a monthly or yearly plan.

What you get

  • Screen and inventory management with availability and booking tracking
  • Campaigns, proposals and invoices for your clients
  • CRM and lead tracking
  • Live operations, proof of play and field photos with GPS check (Professional and above)
  • Client portal, expenses and finance reports (Professional and above)
  • Public marketplace page for your screens (Business and above)
  • API access (Enterprise)
